HOW TO ADD YOUR PROFILE PICTURE:

Option 1 (Recommended):
Simply place your picture in this `assets` folder named `profile.jpg` (or `profile.png`) and update the `src` attribute in `index.html` at line 76:
    <img id="user-avatar" src="assets/profile.jpg" alt="Jay Wilfred Buro">

Option 2 (Instant Preview right on the website):
Open the website (`index.html`) in your browser and click on the circular avatar area (or the "Change Photo" button). You can pick any photo from your computer and it will immediately preview inside the purple circular frame!
